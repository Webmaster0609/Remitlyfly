Privacy Policy

RemitlyFly

Effective date: 9 November 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how RemitlyFly collects, uses, stores and protects information when you use our website and services.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use the platform, we may collect:

  • Account information, such as your email address and authentication details.
  • Recipient information, such as names, phone numbers, countries, relationships and optional notes.
  • Transfer-related information, such as amounts, currencies, displayed exchange-rate estimates, payout methods and transaction history.
  • Technical information needed to operate, secure and troubleshoot the platform.

RemitlyFly does not currently request identity card or passport documents through its website.

3. How We Use Information

We may use information to:

  • Create and manage user accounts.
  • Manage saved recipient details.
  • Display transfer information and estimates.
  • Maintain transaction records.
  • Protect the platform and respond to support requests.

4. Information Sharing

Information may be processed by service providers that help operate the website, authentication and database services. We do not sell personal information.

5. Data Security

We use technical and organisational measures intended to protect information against unauthorised access, loss or misuse. No online system can guarantee absolute security.

6. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, subject to applicable legal requirements.

7. Your Choices and Rights

You may contact us to request access to, correction of or deletion of your personal information, subject to applicable law.

8. Children's Privacy

Our services are not intended for children under 18.
